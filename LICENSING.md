# Licensing

Plain Language Marxist has three kinds of material, and each is licensed separately.

| Material | Where | License |
|---|---|---|
| **Software**: apps, packages, CLI, build scripts | everything outside `content/` and `ingestion/snapshots/` | [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) |
| **Project content**: Plain English renderings, explanations, vocabulary, collections, reading paths | `content/` (except the original texts, see below) | [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE-CONTENT) |
| **Original source texts** | `source.yml` files in `content/works/`, plus `ingestion/snapshots/` | As recorded per work in `work.yml` → `rights` |

## Original source texts

Each work's rights are verified and recorded individually in its `work.yml` (status, attribution, who verified it and when). PLM imports only works whose rights allow redistribution and derivative works: public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or permission granted and recorded. See `docs/architecture/SDD.md` §5.6.

Being on the Marxists Internet Archive does not by itself make a text public domain. MIA states that some of its texts are copyrighted or used with permission.

## Attribution

When reusing PLM content, credit **Plain Language Marxist** and link to the page you used. Keep the source attribution shown on that page as well, for example "Marxists Internet Archive" and the source URL.
