## Goal
Complete Plain English rendering and explanations for Part I of *Das Kapital*, Volume I: Commodities and Money (Chapters 1 to 3).

## Scope
- Chapter 1: Commodities (Use-value, Value, Labour, Form of Value, Commodity Fetishism).
- Chapter 2: Exchange.
- Chapter 3: Money, or the Circulation of Commodities (Measure of values, Medium of circulation, Hoarding, Means of payment, World money).
- This is the theoretical core that beginners find most difficult; Plain English rendering must disentangle Marx's abstract dialectical presentation of the value-form without losing theoretical precision.
