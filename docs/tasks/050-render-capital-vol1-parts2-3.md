## Goal
Complete Plain English rendering and explanations for Parts II and III of *Das Kapital*, Volume I: Transformation of Money into Capital and The Production of Absolute Surplus-Value (Chapters 4 to 11).

## Scope
- Part II: Chapters 4–6 (General Formula for Capital, Contradictions in the General Formula, Buying and Selling of Labour-Power).
- Part III: Chapters 7–11 (The Labour-Process and the Process of Producing Surplus-Value, Constant Capital and Variable Capital, The Rate of Surplus-Value, The Working-Day, Rate and Mass of Surplus-Value).
- Focus: Explaining the secret of surplus-value extraction in the hidden abode of production, and the historical struggle over the working day.
